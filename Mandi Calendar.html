Mandi Calendar

In season

On the stall all year: banana, papaya, coconut, lemon, onion, potato, ginger, curry leaves. Good to know, but not worth waiting for.

Seasons here follow broad climate zones, so states that share a zone share a calendar, and within a large state the hills and the plains can run several weeks apart. Cold storage and imports mean you will still see out-of-season produce — it just costs more and tastes like less.

Calendar compiled by hand from horticulture board and state agriculture extension sources. Corrections welcome; the ones that matter usually come from the person running the stall.

Reading the labels

Every item carries one label telling you where it sits in its season here.

Just arrived
The season has just opened The first of the crop is reaching stalls this month. Prices are usually at their highest now and the pick is small, but this is the freshest it will be all year.
In season
The middle of the run Widely grown nearby and easy to find. This is when it is cheapest, best and most worth building a meal around.
Last month
The season ends here This is the final month it comes off local fields. From next month anything you see has been held in cold storage or trucked in from another zone — it costs more and tastes of less.

Labels come from the growing calendar for your state's climate zone. States that share a zone share a calendar, so within a large state the hills and the plains can differ by several weeks.
